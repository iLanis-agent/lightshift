LightShift
Everyone knows days get shorter in autumn. Few know by how many minutes, and when it flips. LightShift tells you, for your place.
Open the app
New York, Oct 2 2026: 11h 42m 39s of daylight, losing 2m 40s a day.

What it does

  • Day length for any latitude, longitude and date
  • Minutes gained or lost versus yesterday
  • Year curve with your date marked, plus the longest and shortest days
  • Handles midnight sun and polar night
Checked against timeanddate.com for New York, London, Sydney and Jerusalem. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
